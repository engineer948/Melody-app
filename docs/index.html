Melody

A free, open-source music player for your own Jellyfin or Navidrome / Subsonic server. No ads, no tracking, no account with us.

⬇ Download for Android ★ Source on GitHub

Android 7.0 or newer · English, Türkçe, Azərbaycanca · MIT licence

Everything you need, nothing you don't

🎵

Your whole library

Albums, artists, genres, playlists, smart playlists and fast search, from Jellyfin or Navidrome.

📴

Offline

Download songs, albums and playlists. Quality presets for Wi-Fi, mobile data and downloads.

🎤

Synced lyrics

Lyrics that line up with each song by themselves.

🎛️

Sound

Equalizer, volume normalisation, crossfade, sleep timer and Auto DJ.

🚗

Everywhere

Android Auto, a home screen widget and playback on DLNA speakers.

📊

Your year in music

Listening statistics and a Year in Review, worked out on your phone.

🎧

Listen together

Play in sync with other people on your server.

🔒

Private by design

Built-in Tailscale lets friends reach your server with an invite code. Your history never leaves the phone.

Your data stays yours

Melody talks only to your server. There is no Melody account, no analytics and no ads. Read the privacy notes.
