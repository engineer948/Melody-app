# Melody — terms of use and privacy

Version 1. The same text is shown in the app (Settings → About → Terms and privacy) in English, Turkish and Azerbaijani.

## What Melody is

Melody is a free, open-source player for music on sources you choose: your Jellyfin or Subsonic-compatible server, internet radio and files on this phone. It does not sell, host or distribute music; it only plays and processes what your own server or source gives it. You are responsible for having the right to the music on your servers and for how you use the app.

## Sharing clips and videos

Clips, story pictures and videos and cards are made on this phone only when you ask, and sent only through the apps you choose. Melody and its developers do not see, store or send them. Songs and lyrics are usually protected by copyright. Before sharing, make sure you may: you own the rights, have permission, or the law of your country allows it (for example a short quotation, private use or fair use). You alone are responsible for what you share and for any claims arising from it. Clips are limited to 60 seconds, but that limit does not make a share lawful by itself. By confirming the notice before your first share you accept these terms; the date you did so is kept on this phone.

## Privacy

Melody has no account with us, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your listening history, places, memories, letters and settings stay on this phone and in backups you make yourself. Your server address and sign-in go only to your server. Location and photos are read only if you turn on "Soundtrack of your life" and allow them, and they never leave the phone.

## Other services the app can contact

Only when used: your own music servers; LRCLIB (lrclib.net) for lyrics, off by default; the radio-browser.info directory when you browse radio; Tailscale's coordination server when you join with an invite (optional); GitHub to look for new versions (not in the F-Droid build, and it can be turned off). Each has its own terms and privacy policy.

## Questions and complaints

Melody hosts no content, so it cannot remove something someone has shared; please contact the person who shared it or the service where it was posted. Questions about the app itself: github.com/engineer948/Melody-app/issues.
